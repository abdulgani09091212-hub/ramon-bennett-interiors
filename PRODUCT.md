## Product Principles

1. **Artifact & Photograph Lead from the First Pixel:** Never bury interior photography under generic SaaS cards, 3-column icon boxes, or AI-slop gradients. Let full-bleed architectural photography, real archival studio history, and interactive Before/After transformations carry the weight.
2. **Show the Structural Cure, Not Just the Styled Room:** Every case study pairs the raw "Before" spatial problem with the "After" architectural and psychological resolution.
3. **Editorial Restraint Over Gimmicks:** Use heavy, damped cubic-bezier motion (`[0.76, 0, 0.24, 1]`), structured hairlines, and typographic hierarchy rather than bouncy springs or decorative badges.
4. **Effortless High-Ticket Conversion & Ownership:** Make booking a London/Salvador consultation (Cal.com / WhatsApp / Brevo) and updating projects (`/admin`) effortless.
